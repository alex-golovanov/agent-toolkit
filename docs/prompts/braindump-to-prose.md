# Turn a braindump into readable prose

Turn the notes I provide into a readable draft.

First extract the main point, supporting facts, intended audience, and important uncertainties. You
may regroup and reorder the notes for clarity. Remove repetition, but preserve my meaning,
qualifications, and concrete details. Do not add claims, numbers, anecdotes, or conclusions that the
notes do not support. Ask about a gap only when it would materially change the piece; otherwise flag
it separately.

Then write a coherent draft for the audience and format I specify. If I provide an approved voice
profile, use writing-in-style for a final voice pass and run its companion checker when metrics are
available. Otherwise use clear, natural English and say that no personal voice profile was applied.
Prioritize accurate meaning and readability over matching numerical targets.

Return the complete draft, followed by a short note about unresolved questions and any material
changes in structure. Do not publish anything.
